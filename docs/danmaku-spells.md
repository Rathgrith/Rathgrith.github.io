# The Playground spell catalogue

Each opponent runs six original cards on their existing recording. A card's first legal emission starts at its own tick zero, so its signature opening is the same after a declaration or a round change. Interludes begin with a fresh clock after the timed clear. The following cues describe the attacks from the player's side of the field.

| Alice · the sewing theatre | How to read the stitch |
| --- | --- |
| 縫符「針を休める一目」 | Three needles lean together in a slow sweep. The resting needle steps right, then left. Move through the seam it skips. A two-frequency Lissajous stitch leads into offset half-diamonds. Cross to the next idle panel when the other wing starts its half. The final stitch hangs briefly, marking the edge that closes next. An ivory tip and a bobbin frame a hollow feather on each active stitch. |
| 織機「夜行シャトル」 | Two shuttles slide past one another at different heights. Their ribbons alternate. Cross behind the nearer passing ribbon. The shuttles roll out cycloidal ribbons. In the middle they meet at the selvedge and fold away. Alice marks the pilot herself above the folded ribbon. Sidestep the fast needle cluster, then turn along the sparse shoulder between its two irregular trailing tassels. The mark stays where it was set, even as Alice moves. Watch the ivory point on the solid shuttle. It crosses the loom once, then returns lower down. Slip behind its trailing bobbin on the way back. |
| 釦譜「ほどけた留め穴」 | Two incomplete buttonhole loops arrive at different speeds. Find the inward-facing opening before the other loop reaches it. Scalloped epicycloid eyelets interrupt the loops. The two sources then take turns tying the same buttonhole. Its pale ends split apart in the last third, leaving the cut through the lower eyelet open. Two scalloped rows reach the lower field together. Ivory spools mark the opening that travels across the next row. |
| 折符「袖に隠した階段」 | Pins drift, then release in order from left to right. Read the missing pin and take that step before the fold drops. A tilted cycloid draws the pleats. Their release order reverses, then two folded fans unfold from the middle. Watch for the pair of missing pins. Look along the hanging pins before they release. Neighbouring folds slide on opposite slopes. |
| 刺繍「身代わりの型紙」 | A doll traces the pilot's position at launch. Its wide fan then folds toward that location. Lure the tracing to one side and move away. The later dolls pin the pilot’s position as a gap in a two-colour falling hem. Enter the cut and follow it across the cloth. Its seam travels 22 pixels in the middle movement and 49 pixels towards the opposite side in the reprise. A three-petal tracing announces the next panel. The two dolls also fit the handles of a pair of shears around a single sampled position. Lure the hinge to one side and move beyond the blade tips before they cross. |
| 幕間「仕立屋の最後の一針」 | Two narrow beams mark the edges of an open door. Read the beam warnings and follow the door to its next stitch. A five-cusped canopy hangs above the door. The spotlights move to the wings and project diagonal edges. In the development the wing dolls trade spotlight passages for radial rows of thread. The bobbins bracket a skipped length on each ray. Slip back across the threads between them. In the finale each wing gains a third ray and the shared slit changes depth between casts. Three footlights drop tassels at uneven intervals. Each opens a two-colour fan. Move under the spent hinge while the next light prepares its fan. |

| Marisa · the backyard observatory | How to read the experiment |
| --- | --- |
| 露光「盗み撮りの彗星」 | The mint opening records the pilot's position. Both lenses fire two golden exposures at that same point. Sidestep after the first flash. A violet offbeat sweeps across from the opposite lens before the next photograph. A five-petal afterimage marks the camera at the end of its sweep. In the middle, every other exposure holds a 0.48-second sight mark. Marisa fires three narrow comet files into the mark at 154–203 px/s. A slower, uneven band of violet grain trails the burst. The reprise exposes every plate and adds a second scatter pulse while the opposite lens replays the previous position. Bait the mark, step aside, then read the irregular tail before crossing back. Early in the card, the two lenses project bevelled aperture blades. Their prism tips frame the passage beside the print. |
| 反射「夜空のビリヤード」 | Left and right lenses alternate table shots that bank off the arena wall exactly once. Check the returning side before committing. A triangular rolling orbit marks the cushions. Each banked star then splits into a fork. The reprise sends a second return from across the table between the forks. A white cue ball banks first. The rack rebounds from the same cushion and forms a triangle lower down. Let the cue ball pass and cross at the break in the rack. |
| 分光「三色の指名手配」 | Three white comets split at different heights into mint, gold and violet spectra. Watch the nearest unsplit parent. A scalloped spectrum opens from the prism. Its outer bands fold inward in the middle, then split in reverse height order and fan outward. Move through the gap left by the disappearing parent. The three colours separate at the wings of a caustic and cross at its cusp. Follow the travelling spectral window. |
| 航路「箒に借りた重力」 | The lenses sit 74 pixels apart. A train climbs from one lens and circles the other. Its fast head runs ahead of its tail before both escape along their local tangents. Cross the wake of the last gold or mint star. The middle movement tightens the call and response and reverses the order of departure angles. A third, steeper train answers in the finale. Every other pass, two curled comets slow to a stop at different heights. The leader departs first. Cross its wake before the second head cuts the other diagonal. |
| 閃写「星を逃がす暗室」 | Narrow exposures angle towards the two edges of a traveling aisle. The star circle leaves a matching gap. A crossed Lissajous aperture repeats the aisle. The lenses then send three radial files apiece on alternate beats. Each file has two saber tips bracketing seven missing grains. The holes line up on a slope as the first file reaches the lower field. Cut through and reverse through the second lens’s mirrored openings. The last movement brings in a fourth spoke and an earlier reply. The warning beams patrol the outside margins. The pale corners of a perforated print settle in the lower field. Move sideways during the exposure. The sheet then rewinds along its incoming rays. |
| 視差「真夜中の二重像」 | Two cameras photograph the same seven-star dipper in mid-air. Each mint star later passes through the point its gold counterpart occupied, then continues on the opposite diagonal. Follow the leading image through the crossing and leave before the second arrives. The middle movement brings the plate forward and delays the reply. The finale alternates the depth of the plate and slows the second exposure. A pair of offset crescent contours frames an opening on the star chart. |

| Patchouli · the annotated manuscript | Three musical movements |
| --- | --- |
| 索引「余白に棲む栞」 | Follow the missing bookmark in the rings. Opposing pages open around it, then turn into doubled folds. A three-cusped rolling curve marks the next line. Seven elemental inks mark a tilted orbit. Both arcs leave the same moving bookmark clear in the lower field. |
| 傍注「行間を歩くインク」 | Curling ink traces a cycloid. The side volumes draw braces and a small curl in the S-shaped script. From the middle movement, a solar mark records the pilot’s position. Patchouli reads that spot with two fast, narrow quill clusters, then three in the final movement. Lead them aside and turn through the slower, uneven tail of footnotes. A heavy drop arrives before its ink plume opens. Move around the impact point, then cross between the spreading droplets. |
| 正誤「明日の誤植」 | A Lissajous mark opens the correction. Pink type hangs, strikes horizontally through the old word, then pivots toward the page margin. The last edition holds the horizontal stroke longer. The side books proofread in two strokes. The red line activates first, followed by a gold sweep from the other margin. |
| 対訳「同じ月の異なる綴り」 | Unequal fans leave the same aisle. The left and right brackets then exchange pages. Let the leading page cross, then follow the aisle that opens under the two banks. In the reprise the answering spines wait an extra beat fraction. Once per measure group, six radial lines extend from three books. The two crescent tips on each line bracket a missing word. The blanks reach the same height together, alternating left and right with the edition. Cross back between those tips while the writing falls. The paired books read crescent shapes across two different lines. Watch which line reaches you first and change height between them. |
| 脚注「小さな字の逃げ道」 | A rose-curve seal introduces hooks of large, stationary footnote marks. The large orbs at the ends of each hook and seals between them split into a small orb and a quill on opposite sides of the vanished mark. Read the hooks from the first end in the middle movement and from the opposite end in the last. One side book assembles a dagger-shaped footnote in the upper field before the entire mark drops diagonally. |
| 重書「白紙へ還る夜」 | Follow the blank in the overlaid rings. Side volumes inscribe the two halves of an astrolabe, then a star chart. The central book adds its core after the outer contour appears. Two lines of writing descend, then lift a character at a time. Follow the first erased characters through the opening. |

Buttonhole, Basting, Spectrograph, Shutter, Erratum and Palimpsest sometimes write their mathematical figure beside the caster first. Hollow ink develops in order and holds. A small nib shows the firing direction during the last 0.23 seconds. The primary burst becomes solid on a strong beat in the actual recording. Paired figures can release their other half on the following beat. The other twelve cards use direct, turning, splitting and delayed volleys or lens trajectories. The gameplay clock drives the cue in muted play as well. `node tests/danmaku-inscriptions.cjs` checks real release times and collision before and after that cue.

In the developed movements of Shuttle, Exposure and Marginalia, a reticle records the pilot’s position about half a second before the fast burst. The fast rounds pass through the sampled position. A slower tail spreads around that same bearing, with reproducible variation for each run seed. The wing dolls in Curtain Call, the lenses in Shutter and the three desks in Concordance leave consecutive bullets out of their radial lines. The empty interval travels with each line and gives the pilot time to cross it in either direction.

Each successive spell fields another familiar. Alice and Patchouli progress from three to eight, and Marisa from two to seven. Principal casters play the card-specific motif. Recruits answer from the upper rank in turn with selvage stitches, dividing stars or marginal notes. They withdraw for the interlude.

The bullet bodies mark each part of a volley. Alice pins folded hems with sabers, needles and pearls. Marisa's large prism parents break into fine constellations. Patchouli places quills on strokes and seals on pages, with distinct shapes on either side of a footnote fork. Large pearls have a 4.8-pixel collision radius, small pearls 1.7, and saber centres 2.3 in both modes. Shape selection follows the authored position in the row. It adds no extra projectiles. During an inscription, hollow marks still warn before the live bodies appear.

Between cards, Alice's adjacent dolls alternate measuring fans, Marisa uses a sighting fan and a second test exposure, and Patchouli rotates broken lines between the three books. Each interlude adds a density step to its measure. Different stages change the sources, timing, or travel speed. Upper-field colours and projectile silhouettes indicate families, while the opening and motion remain readable in reduced-motion mode.

## Rank and verification

Each card develops and reprises its opening over the three thirds of its actual recording section. The firing clock phrases individual half-beats inside that structure. Rank adds grains and raises the frequency of musical subdivisions. A card's projectile velocities, beam widths, warning times and scripted missing sources stay independent of rank. The highest rank fires subdivisions at 1.28× the opening cadence. Timed splits consume the parent, and the final companion bank in Marisa’s billiards returns off the second cushion.

The density, nonspell, rank and exposure-pressure tests sample actual running games. The density probe uses three pilot positions per opponent across four complete recording cycles and separately checks physical hitboxes and a wider movement margin.
