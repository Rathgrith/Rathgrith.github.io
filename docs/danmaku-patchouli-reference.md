# Patchouli: source inspection and reconstruction

The six spell IDs retain the original **EoSD Normal base-name versions** (Agni Shine, Princess Undine, Sylphy Horn, Lazy Trilithon) and the **Extra** versions (Royal Flare, Philosopher's Stone). They deliberately do not borrow the Hard/Lunatic upgrade attacks under the base names. Timing and counts are rescaled for the site's 240×360 field and music clock; these are visual/mechanical reconstructions, not recovered ECL scripts or frame-identical ports.

## Mechanics cross-checks and implementation limits

The [Stage 4 strategy descriptions](https://wikiwiki.jp/thk/紅/4) corroborate bent fire trajectories, down-left wind pressure and delayed earth redirection. The [Extra descriptions](https://wikiwiki.jp/thk/紅/EX) distinguish the five rice behaviours: fast aimed blue; yellow random redirection; red double radial waves; green redirection toward lower left; pale metal translating an intact formation toward the player.

The footage establishes the shapes and sources; it does not expose exact ECL frame timings, original random seeds, acceleration constants or hitbox sizes. Those values are calibrated for this game's smaller field. Royal Flare's offset loops use a translated velocity-circle construction; this reproduces the observed loop-and-pocket structure but is not a claim about ZUN's original algorithm. Stone motion decelerates continuously, pauses, then redirects. Deterministic pseudo-random samples keep repeated test runs reproducible without making every wave identical.

Undine's original laser volleys contain short travelling beam segments. The shared engine currently supplies stationary warned rays, so the repeated three-way volley is represented with those rays, not a frame-accurate travelling-segment implementation. Three pulses require at most nine simultaneous laser objects, below the twelve-object pool; later water bullets do not add further lasers. The warning and active durations follow the music clock, with the engine's existing minimum warning retained.

The time-attack boss, durability clears and music-driven spell boundaries remain site-specific. A spell begins its internal sequence at tick zero. Later challenge rounds add only a few projectiles to existing formations and increase the shared speed, keeping the original shapes readable.

## Royal Flare production-renderer check

The two full 28-point loops are emitted every four half-beat ticks. The moving centre travels faster than the ring expands, keeping the lower-field loops legible instead of spreading their beads into an indistinct cloud. Existing round orb sprites replace the tiny square pellet sprite for these red beads. Both changes were checked against the production renderer at 4, 8, 14 and 22 seconds of the actual DBU-scored spell phrase, in challenge rounds 1 and 7. The mature field shows several offset loops and their intersections, with visible pockets between them. Peak active bullet counts over those samples were 290 and 274 respectively, below the 720-object pool; the later round moves faster rather than accumulating a larger backlog. No browser errors occurred.

## Sylphy Horn production-renderer check

After the body-trajectory correction, the current source modules and shared production renderer were loaded directly into an isolated browser fixture. At 3 and 4 seconds of the actual first-round DBU phrase, needles visibly bloom around the boss before bending; at 7 and 14 seconds the steeper pale body flow crosses the shallower green boundary flow. This removes the early hard turn that previously made both layers look as though they immediately collapsed toward the same left corner. The backdrop, sprites, music timing, and shared movement integration were the production implementations; no separately drawn mock pattern was used.

The right-boundary source was subsequently checked again in consecutive original frames at **1:08–1:10**. Side needles remain visible close to the lower boundary; restricting their birth positions to the upper two thirds was an implementation error. In the 240×360 field, the source now spans y=18…348 instead of y=18…240. These exact coordinates are field-scaled choices, not recovered original coordinates. The direction remains fixed down-left and independent of player position.

Lower needles (source y≥240) spawn outside the field at x=246, enter at 12 px/s, and smoothly accelerate along the same line after 0.75 seconds. This entrance adjustment provides reaction time in the smaller field without introducing targeted shots or needles appearing on top of the player. A seventh-round motion probe at energy 0.9 measured at least 0.458 seconds between the needle centre crossing the visible right boundary and reaching the nearest possible player hurtbox at x=232.3 across 272 lower-source samples.

The corrected full-height version was rendered again at 3, 4, 7 and 14 seconds: peak active count 259, no browser errors. A separate first-round full-spell run at y=324 checked x=36, 92, 148 and 216. Respectively 35, 32, 23 and 24 distinct bullets intersected a stationary player's hurtbox, so the lower-right region is no longer a permanent shelter. These are trajectory checks with durability clears disabled; they do not claim that every possible point lacks a transient gap.
