# Companion acquaintance dialogue

Reviewed on 2026-10-10. The Japanese remarks in `assets/js/data/companion-remarks.js` are original fan-written dialogue, not quotations or newly established canon. The original 18 base lines remain unchanged. The roster now contains Alice 10, Marisa 14 and Patchouli 10 entries; every entry has a base line and two variants, giving 102 lines in total (84 additions).

The existing friend menu includes acquaintances, interviewers and former opponents. An encounter or a playable route is enough for a restrained impression; it is not evidence of a close friendship, frequent visits, shared private activities or romance. Different game routes are not treated as one verified chronology. Versus-mode quotes are identified separately from story encounters below. The new writing avoids claiming a particular victory, ending or off-screen relationship happened.

## Cross-checks and exclusions

The transcript pages above are community transcriptions/translations of named official works, not publisher-hosted scripts. The Japanese text is used where available. Relationship and story indexes help check context but are not a substitute for a direct encounter: [Alice](https://en.touhouwiki.net/index.php?mobileaction=toggle_view_desktop&title=Alice_Margatroid#Relationships), [Patchouli](https://en.touhouwiki.net/wiki/Patchouli_Knowledge#Relationships), [Yukari](https://en.touhouwiki.net/index.php?title=Yukari_Yakumo#Relationships). The [canon/route discussion](https://en.touhouwiki.net/index.php?mobileaction=toggle_view_desktop&title=Canon) is a further reason not to combine every playable route into one asserted history.

The existing small-circle entries remain. In particular, the Alice/three-fairies context is documented in *Strange and Bright Nature Deity*; Patchouli's Remilia relationship and mansion interactions have substantially stronger support than merely appearing in the same game. Their indexes above were checked to keep the new variants within those existing boundaries.

Koakuma and Flandre were deliberately not added to Patchouli's menu merely to increase its size. Sharing a mansion or appearing as a library midboss does not establish the particular conversations, assistant relationship or daily habits that a casual fan script might otherwise invent. Alice/Reisen also remains out of this revision because the stronger story/interview connections already meet the intended roster size. No shipping index, unofficial game or fan comic is used as evidence of an original-work acquaintance.

## Voice and direction

Alice is practical, observant and quietly competitive; craft remarks vary between control, layout and unfinished research rather than repeatedly saying she repairs dolls. Marisa uses informal speech and occasional `ぜ` / `だぜ`, without appending it to every sentence. Patchouli is concise, dry and interested in causes; she can acknowledge a mistaken hypothesis instead of always being the authority. New remarks do not introduce confessions or insist that opponents are close friends.

All 102 lines have an explicit face ID, pose ID and effect. Most new lines use `none`; the remaining accents are chosen for their local meaning, such as an evasive sweat drop, a research idea, a question or an exasperated sigh. No new line uses `sparkle`. Variants are complete line objects so their direction is not inherited accidentally from a different joke or mood. IDs name the subject and are unique within each speaker's list; no list contains its own speaker.
