# Character squadron insignia

Three original flat high-resolution insignia generated on 2026-10-10 with the built-in imagegen tool. The designs use bold color fields and distinct silhouettes without metallic detail. The full transparent PNGs are used directly, with no pixel conversion. The final prompts are saved in `prompts.json`.

- `alice.png`: an elongated puppet-and-ribbon heraldic lozenge, blue and ivory with red ties.
- `marisa.png`: a sweeping asymmetric witch hat, broom and star with gold flight trails.
- `patchouli.png`: a round crescent and astrolabe surrounding an open grimoire, silver and violet.

Each design has its own silhouette and composition. The brief references the individuality of aviation squadron insignia from Ace Combat, without copying an existing squadron's mark. These replace both the old dithered still lifes and the unused pixel-crest draft.

`classic-theme.css` selects the illustration; `classic-ornaments.css` controls its size, placement and restrained contrast. The complete images appear as decorative corner watermarks at 240px on desktop and 160px on compact screens and companion panels. Empty side rails contain no artwork. Decorative backgrounds are disabled for print and forced-color views.
