Adaptation: Simplified to 0.25 degree tolerance, projected as equirectangular
(longitude + 180, 90 - latitude) into a 360 x 180 SVG viewBox, with a
monochrome dot-matrix land fill. No political borders are represented.
For overlays, x = (longitude + 180) / 360 * width and
y = (90 - latitude) / 180 * height.
